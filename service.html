Little questions

How far ahead should I book wedding flowers?

Ideally 6–9 months ahead, though we often fit in shorter notice. Peak dates go first.

Can I request specific flowers out of season?

Yes — we'll source what we can, be upfront about cost, and suggest in-season look-alikes.

How do I make my bouquet last longer?

Trim stems at an angle, change the water daily, use the flower food provided and keep away from direct sun.

Where do you deliver?

Across the city and surrounding suburbs. Wider areas are available for weddings and events with a travel fee.
